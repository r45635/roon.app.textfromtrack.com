## In one line

> Synced lyrics for everything Roon plays — line by line, full screen, on your phone or your TV.

## In a paragraph

TextFromTrack Roon Companion is a free, open-source Roon extension that shows the lyrics of
whatever a zone plays, line by line and in time with the music — streaming from Qobuz or TIDAL,
radio or local files. Click a line and Roon jumps there; nudge the timing when a version runs
early; go full screen, or turn a phone, a tablet or a TV browser into a lyrics display that
follows its own zone. It needs no music folder and no account. With access to your library, it
can also write lyrics into your files, fill a whole library in one batch, and generate AI lyrics
for the songs no one has transcribed.

## The longer story

Roon gives you rich album pages, credits and reviews, but no synced lyrics for most tracks. The
companion fills that gap. It pairs with the Roon Core like any extension, follows the zones in
real time and fetches time-stamped lyrics from LRCLIB, a free community database, the moment a
track starts — then keeps them, so the next play is instant. It runs where you want it: as a
desktop app in the menu bar or system tray, or as a Docker container on a NAS, with a web
interface every device in the house can open. Only a track's title, artist, album and length
leave your network to find its lyrics; a screenshot mode hides every personal name on screen.
