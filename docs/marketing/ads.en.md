## Roon Community — "Tinkering" / extensions

**Title:** TextFromTrack Roon Companion — synced lyrics for everything Roon plays (free, open source)

> Hi all,
>
> I've been building a small Roon extension that shows **synced lyrics** for whatever a zone is
> playing — Qobuz, TIDAL, radio or your own files. The current line is highlighted and follows the
> music; click a line and Roon jumps there.
>
> **What it does**
> - Lyrics for every track, line by line, with a timing nudge (±0.5 s) when a version runs early or late
> - Full-screen view, and a lyrics display page for a tablet or TV (each screen can follow its own zone)
> - Zone menu, transport, volume — so it also works as a simple remote with lyrics
> - Optional, if you point it at your music folder: write `.lrc` files or embed lyrics in your tags,
>   fill your whole library in one batch, sync plain lyrics yourself, or generate them with AI
>
> **What it needs**
> Nothing on the Roon side beyond enabling the extension. No music folder, no account.
> Lyrics come from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free community database.
>
> **Install** — desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux) or Docker on a NAS (amd64 and arm64):
> https://github.com/r45635/roon.app.textfromtrack.com/releases/latest — quick start:
> https://github.com/r45635/roon.app.textfromtrack.com/blob/main/docs/quickstart.md
>
> It's free and open source (MIT). Feedback, bug reports and ideas very welcome — GitHub issues or
> this thread.
>
> *[AD2 image] [AD5 clip]*
> *Screenshots: songs by Josh Woodward (CC BY 4.0). Not affiliated with Roon Labs.*

### r/selfhosted
**Title:** Roon lyrics companion — Docker image (amd64/arm64) that shows synced lyrics on any screen in the house

> Small Node service + web UI that pairs with your Roon Core and shows synced lyrics for whatever
> is playing. Runs fine on a NAS (`network_mode: host` on Linux), no volume needed for lyrics.
> Phone/tablet/TV display via a QR code, optional `ACCESS_TOKEN` for other devices, `UPDATE_CHECK=false`
> to stay offline from GitHub. Mount your music folder (`:rw`) only if you want it to write lyrics
> into your files.
>
> `ghcr.io/r45635/roon-companion` · https://github.com/r45635/roon.app.textfromtrack.com
